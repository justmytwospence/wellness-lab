# Wellness Lab

Open-source, local-first analytics for Garmin sleep, HRV and health data, for people who want the details: full-resolution nights, cited and adjustable methods, per-metric reliability, event studies, and an inbox where the app proposes illness, alcohol, travel and altitude episodes for you to confirm or deny.

Status: under construction.
